UMP = provincial floor. Many regencies/cities set a higher UMK — employers must pay whichever is greater. Sectoral minimums (UMSP/UMSK) may also apply. Source: PP 49/2025; provincial governor decrees issued Dec 2025. Effective 1 January 2026.

📜Employment Contracts

  • PKWT (Fixed-Term): maximum 5 years total including all extensions, for project-based or seasonal work (UU Cipta Kerja).
  • PKWTT (Permanent): probation maximum 3 months; probation cannot be applied to PKWT employees.
  • All contracts must be in Bahasa Indonesia; bilingual versions allowed but Indonesian text governs.
  • Outsourcing arrangements must comply with Permenaker No. 11/2019 on permitted job categories.
  • UMP/UMK applies only to employees with less than 1 year of experience — senior staff wages are set by negotiation above the minimum.

⏰Working Hours & Overtime

  • Standard: 8 hrs/day, 40 hrs/week (5-day week) or 7 hrs/day, 40 hrs/week (6-day week).
  • Overtime cap: 4 hrs/day and 18 hrs/week (Permenaker 5/2023 — increased from previous 14 hrs/week).
  • Weekday overtime pay: 1.5× hourly rate for the 1st hour; 2× for each subsequent hour.
  • Weekend/public holiday: 2× for the first 8 hrs; 3× for the 9th hr; 4× for the 10th+ hrs.
  • Overtime must be agreed in writing by the employee; mandatory rest of 30 mins after 4 consecutive overtime hours.

🌴Leave Entitlements (Pasal 79 & 81)

  • Annual leave (Cuti Tahunan): 12 working days after 12 months of continuous service.
  • Maternity: 3 months total (1.5 months before + 1.5 months after delivery, or 6 weeks before + 6 weeks after).
  • Paternity: 2 days paid for wife's delivery; 2 days for wife's miscarriage.
  • Menstrual leave: 2 days for the 1st and 2nd day of cycle — employee need not work if reporting pain (Pasal 81).
  • Marriage: 3 days; Bereavement (immediate family): 2 days; Child's marriage/circumcision/baptism: 1 day; Pilgrimage (Haj): once, for the duration.

💰UMP 2026 — Key Rates for Business Hubs

  • DKI Jakarta: Rp 5,729,876/month — highest in Indonesia, up 6.17% (Rp +333,115) from 2025. Set by Kepgub DKI Jakarta No. 1142/2025.
  • Jawa Barat (West Java): Rp 2,317,601 (+5.77%) — UMK in Bekasi, Karawang and Depok significantly higher.
  • Banten: Rp 3,100,881 (+6.74%) — covers Tangerang area industrial zones.
  • Bali: Rp 3,207,459 (+7.04%); East Java: Rp 2,446,880 (+6.5%).
  • New formula under PP 49/2025: UMP = prior UMP × (1 + Inflation + (GDP growth × Alpha)), Alpha 0.50–0.90. Decentralised to regional wage councils.

🏥BPJS Contributions 2026 — Updated Ceilings

  • BPJS Kesehatan: 4% employer + 1% employee. Salary cap: Rp 12,000,000/month (max employer contribution: Rp 480,000; max employee: Rp 120,000).
  • JHT (Jaminan Hari Tua): 3.7% employer + 2% employee — no salary cap.
  • JP (Jaminan Pensiun): 2% employer + 1% employee. Ceiling updated to Rp 11,086,300/month effective March 2026 (up from Rp 10,547,400 in 2025), following 5.11% GDP growth. Max employer contribution: Rp 221,726; max employee: Rp 110,863.
  • JKK (Jaminan Kecelakaan Kerja): 0.24%–1.74% employer only, by workplace risk category.
  • JKM (Jaminan Kematian): 0.3% employer only. New JKP (Jaminan Kehilangan Pekerjaan) funded 0.22% by government + 0.14% recomposed from JKK — no new payroll line for employers (PP 6/2025).

🎁THR — Tunjangan Hari Raya

  • Mandatory for all employees — both PKWTT (permanent) and PKWT (fixed-term).
  • Amount: 1 month gross salary for employees with ≥12 months continuous service; prorated (months ÷ 12 × 1 month salary) for <12 months.
  • Payment deadline: at least 7 days before the employee's religious holiday (Eid al-Fitr, Christmas, Nyepi, Waisak, etc.).
  • Penalty for late payment: Rp 5,000,000 fine + 5% surcharge on the THR amount owed (Permenaker 6/2016).
  • Includes religious allowances and regular fixed benefits in the gross calculation base.

💼Severance & Termination (PP 35/2021)

  • Uang Pesangon (Severance): up to 9× monthly salary for PKWTT employees based on tenure (Pasal 40 PP 35/2021).
  • Uang Penghargaan Masa Kerja (Long Service): 2–10× monthly salary for ≥3 years continuous service.
  • Uang Penggantian Hak: 15% of (pesangon + penghargaan) for housing/medical + untaken annual leave payout + repatriation costs.
  • PHK (termination) requires bipartite negotiation first, then Mediasi P2 if unresolved. Employees can contest via Industrial Relations Court (PHI).
  • Resignation by employee: entitled to Penggantian Hak only (no pesangon), unless specific conditions under PP 35/2021 apply.

📋PPh 21 — Employee Income Tax 2026

  • PTKP (Non-taxable threshold): Rp 54,000,000/yr (TK/0 — single); +Rp 4,500,000 married (K/0); +Rp 4,500,000 per dependent, max 3 (up to K/3: Rp 72,000,000/yr).
  • Biaya Jabatan (occupational cost deduction): 5% of gross, capped at Rp 500,000/month (Rp 6,000,000/yr) — deducted before tax calculation.
  • Progressive tax brackets: 5% (Rp 0–60M), 15% (Rp 60–250M), 25% (Rp 250–500M), 30% (Rp 500M–5B), 35% (>Rp 5B annual taxable income).
  • Freelancers / non-employees: PPh 21 on 50% of gross fee at progressive rates (Norma Penghitungan).
  • Monthly reporting via e-Bupot 21/26 (Coretax system); SPT Masa due by the 20th of the following month. Annual SPT due 31 March (individuals).
